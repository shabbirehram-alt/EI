About your compass

Methods, privacy and responsible use

Designed for reflection and action

This tool combines established ideas from emotional intelligence, emotion regulation, assertive communication, cognitive reframing, structured feedback, self-compassion, and goal implementation.

Your privacy
Your name, answers, notes, and progress are stored locally in this browser. They are not transmitted by this file.

Knowledge foundations

Daniel Goleman’s emotional intelligence domains; Mayer, Salovey and Caruso’s ability model; James Gross’s emotion-regulation process model; Marshall Rosenberg’s Observation, Feeling, Need, Request structure; Center for Creative Leadership’s Situation, Behavior, Impact feedback model; Gabriele Oettingen’s WOOP method; Kristin Neff’s self-compassion work; and implementation-intention research associated with Peter Gollwitzer.

Important limits

This is a guided self-reflection tool, not a validated psychometric test, diagnosis, therapy, legal advice, or a substitute for organizational policy. If a situation involves harassment, discrimination, threats, violence, coercion, self-harm, or immediate danger, prioritize safety and contact an appropriate trusted person, organizational reporting channel, qualified professional, or local emergency service.
